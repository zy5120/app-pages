常见问题

Q: 如何选择起课方式？

重大事宜推荐使用正时起课（古籍正统算法）；日常小事可使用报数或汉字起课；单双数仅适合粗略速断。

Q: 起课结果是否准确？

小鱼六壬忠实还原《玉匣记》李淳风六壬时课古籍算法。但请理性看待，课式结果仅供文化学习与娱乐参考。

Q: 历史记录存在哪里？

仅存储在您的设备本地。删除应用或手动清空记录即可永久清除。

Q: 应用是否需要联网？

起课核心功能可离线使用。AI 解读、追问与重新生成需要联网；内置 AI 使用 DeepSeek，无需个人密钥，自备 AI 直连您配置的服务商。Apple 商品查询、购买、恢复与权益更新也可能需要联网。

Q: 每日免费额度如何计算？

内置 AI 每天免费 1 次，本机按北京时间每天 0 点重置。解读、追问或重新生成只要返回内容即计 1 次；失败且没有内容不扣次数。

Q: 月订阅和永久版有什么区别？

内置 AI 月订阅：每期 1 个月、自动续订，中国大陆 ¥10/月；有效期内不限次数使用内置 AI，也可配置自己的 AI 密钥。自备 AI 永久版：一次性非消耗性购买，中国大陆 ¥48；永久解锁自备 AI，需您提供服务商密钥，不包含内置 AI 无限使用，仍享每天 1 次免费内置 AI。实际当地价格以购买页面显示为准，自备 AI 服务商可能另行收费。

小鱼六壬和小鱼塔罗分别购买，权益不互通；当前不提供免费试用或家庭共享。入口为「更多 → AI 会员」，AI 配置与受限结果页也可进入会员页。

Q: 如何配置自备 AI？

先确认拥有有效月订阅或自备 AI 永久版，再到「更多 → AI 助手」新增模型，填写服务商地址、个人 API Key 与模型名称，保存并选择模型。个人密钥保存在本机，仅用于向您配置的服务地址认证。使用内置 AI 无需完成这一步。

Q: 如何恢复购买或管理订阅？

在「更多 → AI 会员」选择「恢复购买」，并使用原购买时的 Apple 账户。应用通过 StoreKit 2 验证当前权益；到期、退款或撤销会依 Apple 返回的有效状态更新访问权限，无需注册开发者账号。

月订阅会自动续订，可在会员页管理订阅，或打开 iPhone/iPad「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」取消。取消续订后可使用至已付费周期结束；删除应用不会自动取消订阅。永久版不按月续费。

Does the app need an internet connection?

Core casting features work offline. AI readings, follow-ups, and regeneration require a connection. Built-in AI uses DeepSeek without a personal key; own-key AI connects directly to your configured provider. Apple product lookup, purchases, restoration, and entitlement updates may also require a connection.

How does the daily free allowance work?

Built-in AI includes 1 free request per day, reset on-device at midnight Beijing time. A reading, follow-up, or regeneration counts once when it returns content. A failure with no content does not count.

What is the difference between monthly and lifetime access?

Built-in AI Monthly is an auto-renewing subscription with a 1-month period, CNY 10/month in mainland China. While active, it includes unlimited built-in AI and access to your own AI key. Own AI Lifetime is a one-time non-consumable purchase, CNY 48 in mainland China. It permanently unlocks own-key AI, for which you must supply a provider key. It does not include unlimited built-in AI; the daily free built-in request remains available. Local prices are shown on the purchase screen. Your AI provider may charge separately.

Purchases are separate for Fish Liu Ren and Fish Tarot. No free trial or Family Sharing is currently offered. Open More → AI Membership; AI settings and restricted result pages also link to membership.

How do I configure my own AI?

With an active monthly subscription or lifetime purchase, open More → AI Assistant and add a model using your provider's service address, personal API key, and model name, then save and select it. The key stays on-device and is used only to authenticate with your configured service address. Built-in AI does not require this setup.

How do I restore purchases or manage a subscription?

Open More → AI Membership → Restore Purchases using the same Apple account used for the purchase. StoreKit 2 verifies current access, which is updated based on Apple's valid entitlement status after expiration, refunds, or revocation. No developer account is required.

The monthly subscription renews automatically. Manage it from the membership page or open iPhone/iPad Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions to cancel. After cancellation, access continues through the paid period. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase has no monthly renewal.
