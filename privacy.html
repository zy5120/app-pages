小鱼六壬 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月18日

数据收集与本地存储

小鱼六壬无需注册账号，不要求您提供姓名、邮箱或手机号。起课历史记录及备注保存在您的设备本地，不会上传至开发者自建服务器。您主动使用 AI 时，相关内容会按下文说明发送给 AI 服务商。

  • 本地数据包括历史记录、AI 解读和追问记录、个人 AI 配置，以及每日免费额度的日期与使用次数。
  • 免费内置 AI 每天 1 次，本机按北京时间每天 0 点重置。解读、追问或重新生成返回内容即计 1 次；失败且没有内容不扣次数。额度记录不通过开发者账号或服务器同步。

网络使用与 AI 服务

核心功能可离线使用。您主动请求 AI 解读、追问或重新生成时，应用会发送当前卦象、课式与起课方式等上下文、您填写的问题；追问还会发送此前的 AI 回复及问答上下文，以便延续对话。

  • 内置 AI：使用 DeepSeek 服务，无需您自行提供 API Key；请求由设备直接发送至 DeepSeek。
  • 自备 AI：使用您配置的服务商和服务地址。个人 API Key 保存在本机，仅用于向您配置的服务地址进行认证，不发送给开发者自建服务器。
  • AI 请求由客户端直接发送给相应服务商，不经过开发者的 AI 代理。服务商如何处理、保存和删除收到的数据，请以该服务商的隐私政策为准。
  • App Store 商品查询、购买、恢复购买和权益更新也可能使用网络，不取决于是否配置个人 AI 密钥。

Apple 购买与权益

支付由 Apple 处理。应用通过 StoreKit 2 读取并验证 Apple 提供的交易及当前权益信息，包括商品标识、有效期和撤销状态，用于解锁功能、恢复购买，并在订阅到期、退款或撤销后更新相应权益。购买验证不经过开发者自建后端。

月订阅在有效期内提供无限次内置 AI，并允许使用自备 AI；永久版为非消耗性购买，仅永久解锁自备 AI，不包含无限次内置 AI，仍保留每日 1 次免费内置 AI。小鱼六壬与小鱼塔罗分别购买，权益不互通。具体价格及管理方法见支持页面。

Apple 按其隐私政策处理 App Store 与支付服务的信息。

第三方服务

本应用不使用第三方分析、广告或跟踪组件。使用的第三方服务包括上述内置或自备 AI 服务，以及 Apple 的 App Store 与 StoreKit 服务。

数据安全与删除

本机记录、配置及额度数据受 iOS 系统沙盒保护。您可在应用内删除历史记录；删除应用会移除该应用的本机数据，但不会自动取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除 Apple 持有的购买记录或 AI 服务商已收到的内容。有效购买可通过 Apple 账户恢复；第三方数据的处理与删除适用相应服务商的规则。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，亦不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

Fish Liu Ren Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Data and Local Storage

Fish Liu Ren requires no app account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Casting history and notes are saved on your device and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. When you choose to use AI, relevant content is sent to the AI provider as described below.

  • Local data includes reading history, AI responses and follow-up conversations, personal AI settings, and the date and count used for the daily free allowance.
  • Built-in AI includes 1 free request per day, reset on-device at midnight Beijing time. A reading, follow-up, or regeneration counts once when it returns content; a failure with no content does not count. The allowance is not synchronized through a developer account or server.

Network Use and AI Providers

Core features work offline. When you request an AI reading, follow-up, or regeneration, the app sends the current divination result, chart, and casting-method context and your question. Follow-ups also include previous AI responses and question-and-answer context to continue the conversation.

  • Built-in AI uses DeepSeek. You do not need to supply a personal API key; requests go directly from your device to DeepSeek.
  • Own-key AI uses the provider and service address you configure. Your personal API key is stored on-device and used only to authenticate with that configured service address, not sent to a developer-operated server.
  • AI requests go directly from the client to the relevant provider, without a developer-operated AI proxy. Refer to that provider's privacy policy for how it handles, retains, and deletes received data.
  • App Store product lookup, purchases, restoration, and entitlement updates may also use the network, whether or not you configure a personal AI key.

Apple Purchases and Access

Apple processes payments. The app uses StoreKit 2 to read and verify transactions and current entitlements supplied by Apple, including product identifiers, expiration dates, and revocation status. This information unlocks features, restores purchases, and updates access after expiration, refunds, or revocation. There is no developer-operated purchase verification backend.

An active monthly subscription includes unlimited built-in AI and access to own-key AI. The lifetime non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks own-key AI; it does not include unlimited built-in AI, and the daily free built-in request remains available. Purchases are separate for Fish Liu Ren and Fish Tarot. See the support page for prices and management options.

Apple handles App Store and payment information under its Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking components. Third-party services include the built-in or own-key AI providers described above and Apple's App Store and StoreKit services.

Security and Deletion

Local records, settings, and allowance data are protected by the iOS app sandbox. You can delete reading history in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, but does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription or delete Apple's purchase records or content already received by an AI provider. Valid purchases can be restored through your Apple account. Third-party data handling and deletion are governed by the relevant provider's policies.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
