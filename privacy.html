小鱼六壬 隐私政策

最后更新：2026年11月26日

数据收集与本地存储

小鱼六壬无需注册账号，不要求您提供姓名、邮箱或手机号。起课历史记录及备注默认保存在您的设备上，不会上传至开发者自建服务器。您主动使用 AI 时，相关内容会按下文说明发送给 AI 服务商。

  • 本地数据包括历史记录、AI 解读和追问记录、个人 AI 配置，以及每日免费额度的日期与使用次数、已购解读余额及用于防止重复入账的交易标识。
  • 免费内置 AI 每天 1 次，本机按北京时间每天 0 点重置。解读、追问或重新生成返回内容即计 1 次；失败且没有内容不扣次数。每日免费额度由设备记录。在支持 iCloud 同步的版本中，购买次数包的余额、交易标识和扣次记录保存在用户的私人 iCloud 数据库，用于核对余额和防止重复入账。此账本不包含问题、课式、牌阵、解读或个人密钥。

会员如何同步个人内容？

在支持 iCloud 同步的版本中，有效订阅或股东会员可以自行开启内容同步，将历史问题、解读与追问、草稿、备注及偏好保存在私人 iCloud 中。此选项默认关闭；个人服务密钥留在设备上。关闭内容同步不会关闭已购次数的自动同步。旧版的记录与余额仍按原方式保存在设备上。

网络使用与 AI 服务

核心功能可离线使用。您主动请求 AI 解读、追问或重新生成时，应用会发送当前卦象、课式与起课方式等上下文、您填写的问题；追问还会发送此前的 AI 回复及问答上下文，以便延续对话。

  • 内置 AI：使用 DeepSeek 服务，无需您自行提供 API Key；请求由设备直接发送至 DeepSeek。
  • 自备 AI：使用您配置的服务商和服务地址。个人 API Key 保存在本机，仅用于向您配置的服务地址进行认证，不发送给开发者自建服务器。
  • AI 请求由客户端直接发送给相应服务商，不经过开发者的 AI 代理。服务商如何处理、保存和删除收到的数据，请以该服务商的隐私政策为准。
  • App Store 商品查询、购买、恢复购买和权益更新也可能使用网络，不取决于是否配置个人 AI 密钥。

服务商的数据处理

DeepSeek 的开放平台隐私政策自 2026 年 11 月 11 日生效。根据该政策，输入和输出可能保留并用于模型训练、评估与改进；服务商还处理 API 用量、请求及安全诊断日志，并可按 IP 推断大致位置用于安全。保存期限取决于处理目的和法律要求，本应用不承诺固定删除天数。

自备 AI 的认证凭证对应您在服务商的账户。查询模型列表或余额时，也会向该服务地址发出认证请求，但不会发送卦象、牌阵或问题内容。自备服务商的数据规则以其实际政策为准，请在使用前阅读；本应用不替该服务商承诺不留存或不训练。

AI 数据分享许可

首次主动使用尚未许可的 AI 服务地址（包括更换为新地址）时，应用会说明接收方和发送范围，由您选择是否同意。不同意不影响离线功能。您可在「更多 → 数据与隐私」选择「撤回所有 AI 数据发送许可」；再次主动请求前需重新同意。未获许可时，自动余额查询会跳过，不会联网。

撤回会阻止后续未获许可的 AI、模型列表及余额请求，但不表示已删除服务商此前收到的内容。如需申请访问或删除这类数据，请通过下方已公布的联系渠道提出请求；使用自备 AI 时，也可按服务商政策联系其处理。

Apple 购买与权益

支付由 Apple 处理。应用通过 StoreKit 2 读取并验证 Apple 提供的交易及当前权益信息，包括商品标识、有效期和撤销状态，用于解锁功能、恢复购买，并在订阅到期、退款或撤销后更新相应权益。购买验证不经过开发者自建后端。

有效月度订阅提供不限次内置解读，并允许接入自备服务。股东会员一次性永久解锁自备服务。可重复购买的消耗型次数包每包增加 10 次内置解读。当日免费次数优先，订阅有效时保留已购余额。已购次数在本 App 内长期有效。支持 iCloud 同步的版本可在使用同一 iCloud 账户和同一 App Store 购买账户的设备间同步余额。旧版仅在原设备保存余额。具体价格与管理方式见支持页面。

Apple 按其隐私政策处理 App Store 与支付服务的信息。

第三方服务

本应用不使用第三方分析、广告或跟踪组件。使用的第三方服务包括上述内置或自备 AI 服务，以及 Apple 的 App Store、StoreKit 以及在支持同步的版本中使用的 iCloud 服务。

数据安全与删除

本机记录、配置及额度数据受 iOS 系统沙盒保护。您可在应用内删除历史记录；删除应用会移除历史记录等应用内数据，但不会自动取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除 Apple 持有的购买记录或 AI 服务商已收到的内容。有效订阅和股东会员可通过原 Apple 账户恢复。使用支持 iCloud 同步的版本时，卸载 App 不会自动删除已同步的余额；第三方数据的处理与删除适用相应服务商的规则。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，亦不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

Fish Liu Ren Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Data and Local Storage

Fish Liu Ren requires no app account and does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Casting history and notes are saved on your device by default and are not uploaded to a developer-operated server. When you choose to use AI, relevant content is sent to the AI provider as described below.

  • Local data includes reading history, AI responses and follow-up conversations, personal AI settings, the date and count for the daily free allowance, the purchased credit balance, and transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate credit delivery.
  • Built-in AI includes 1 free request per day, reset on-device at midnight Beijing time. A reading, follow-up, or regeneration counts once when it returns content; a failure with no content does not count. The device tracks the daily free allowance. In versions with iCloud sync, the purchased balance, transaction identifiers and credit usage records are stored in your private iCloud database to reconcile credits and prevent duplicate delivery. The credit ledger contains no questions, spreads, casting results, readings or personal API keys.

Can I sync my reading history and drafts?

In versions with iCloud sync, subscribers and Lifetime Patrons can choose to sync their reading history, questions, follow-ups, drafts, notes and preferences to their private iCloud database. This setting is off by default. Personal provider keys stay on the device. Turning off content sync is independent of automatic purchased-credit syncing. Older versions continue to use local storage.

Network Use and AI Providers

Core features work offline. When you request an AI reading, follow-up, or regeneration, the app sends the current divination result, chart, and casting-method context and your question. Follow-ups also include previous AI responses and question-and-answer context to continue the conversation.

  • Built-in AI uses DeepSeek. You do not need to supply a personal API key; requests go directly from your device to DeepSeek.
  • Own-key AI uses the provider and service address you configure. Your personal API key is stored on-device and used only to authenticate with that configured service address, not sent to a developer-operated server.
  • AI requests go directly from the client to the relevant provider, without a developer-operated AI proxy. Refer to that provider's privacy policy for how it handles, retains, and deletes received data.
  • App Store product lookup, purchases, restoration, and entitlement updates may also use the network, whether or not you configure a personal AI key.

Provider Data Handling

DeepSeek's Open Platform Privacy Policy, effective November 11, 2026, permits retaining inputs and outputs and using them for model training, evaluation, and improvement. It also covers API usage, request and security logs, and approximate location inferred from IP for security. Retention depends on purpose and legal requirements; this app does not promise a fixed deletion period.

Own-key credentials identify your provider account. Model-list and balance lookups also send authenticated requests to that service address, without your question, chart, or spread content. Read your chosen provider's policy before use; this app does not promise that another provider will avoid retention or training.

Permission to Share AI Data

When you first use an AI service address that has not been permitted, including switching to a new address, the app explains the recipient and data being sent and asks for your permission. Declining does not affect offline features. You can withdraw all AI data-sharing permissions in More → Data & Privacy; a later request you initiate requires permission again. Automatic balance checks are skipped without network access when permission is absent.

Withdrawal prevents future AI, model-list, and balance requests without permission. It does not delete content a provider has already received. To request access or deletion, use the published contact details below; for own-key AI, you may also follow your provider's request process.

Apple Purchases and Access

Apple processes payments. The app uses StoreKit 2 to read and verify transactions and current entitlements supplied by Apple, including product identifiers, expiration dates, and revocation status. This information unlocks features, restores purchases, and updates access after expiration, refunds, or revocation. There is no developer-operated purchase verification backend.

An active monthly subscription includes unlimited built-in readings and access to your own provider. A one-time Lifetime Patron purchase permanently unlocks your own provider. Each consumable pack adds 10 built-in readings. Daily free access is used first, and active subscriptions preserve the credit balance. Credits do not expire and belong to this app. Versions with iCloud sync share the balance across devices using the same iCloud account and App Store purchase account. Earlier versions keep the balance on the original device. See the support page for purchase options.

Apple handles App Store and payment information under its Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking components. Third-party services include the built-in or own-key AI providers described above and Apple’s App Store and StoreKit services, plus iCloud in versions that support balance syncing.

Security and Deletion

Local records, settings, and allowance data are protected by the iOS app sandbox. You can delete reading history in the app. Deleting the app removes data such as local reading history, but does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription or delete Apple's purchase records or content already received by an AI provider. Active subscriptions and Lifetime Patron access can be restored through the original Apple account. Removing the app does not automatically delete balances already synced to your private iCloud database. Third-party data handling and deletion are governed by the relevant provider's policies.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
