常见问题

Q: 应用是否需要联网？

抽牌、牌义查阅与历史记录可离线使用。AI 解读、追问与重新生成需要联网；从构建 212 起，内置解读经开发者运营的 api.yubo.net 核对额度，并将本次问题及所需上下文转给 DeepSeek。较早构建由设备直接连接 DeepSeek。自备服务从设备直连您配置的服务商。Apple 商品查询、购买、恢复与权益更新也可能需要联网。

Q: 每日免费额度如何计算？

内置 AI 每天免费 1 次，按北京时间每天 0 点重置。解读、追问或重新生成只要返回内容即计 1 次；失败且没有内容不扣次数。

有哪些会员方案和解读次数包？

月度会员按月自动续订，购买前可查看所在商店的实际价格。订阅有效期间，内置解读和追问不限次数，也可使用自备服务。

股东会员一次购买，永久使用自备服务，另享每天一次免费内置解读。股东同时持有有效月度会员时，会获得「知遇股东」称号。订阅结束后，股东权益继续有效。自备服务需自行提供密钥，服务商可能另行收费。

每包 10 次，可重复购买。在使用中转服务的版本中，有效订阅不限次。股东会员每天享一次免费内置解读，用完可切换至自备服务。两类会员均保留已购次数。免费版先用每日免费次数，用完才可使用已购次数。会员状态无法核实时暂停扣次。已购次数在本 App 内长期有效。支持 iCloud 同步的版本可在使用同一 iCloud 账户和同一 App Store 购买账户的设备间同步余额。旧版仅在原设备保存余额。各地区价格以 Apple 购买页面显示为准。

小鱼六壬和小鱼塔罗分别购买，权益不互通；当前不提供免费试用或家庭共享。入口为「更多 → 本 App 的会员页」，AI 配置与受限结果页也可进入会员页。

会员如何同步个人内容？

在支持 iCloud 同步的版本中，有效订阅或股东会员可以自行开启内容同步，将历史问题、解读与追问、草稿、备注及偏好保存在私人 iCloud 中。此选项默认关闭；个人服务密钥留在设备上。关闭内容同步不会关闭已购次数的自动同步。旧版的记录与余额仍按原方式保存在设备上。

Q: 如何配置自备 AI？

先确认拥有有效订阅或股东会员，再到「更多 → AI 助手」新增模型，填写服务商地址、个人 API Key 与模型名称，保存并选择模型。个人密钥保存在本机，仅用于向您配置的服务地址认证。使用内置 AI 无需完成这一步。

Q: AI 数据会发送到哪里，如何撤回许可？

首次主动使用尚未许可的 AI 服务地址（包括更换为新地址）时，先查看应用显示的接收方及数据范围，再选择是否同意。解读与追问会发送问题及必要上下文；查询模型和余额也会发送服务商账号凭证进行认证。拒绝或撤回不影响离线功能。

在「更多 → 数据与隐私」选择「撤回所有 AI 数据发送许可」，之后主动请求需要重新同意；没有许可的自动余额查询会跳过，不会联网。撤回不等于删除服务商已收到的内容；如需删除，请使用本页联系渠道，或联系您自备 AI 的服务商。数据保存与可能的模型训练说明见隐私政策。

Q: 如何恢复购买或管理订阅？

用购买时的 Apple 账户打开「更多」中的会员页，选择「恢复购买」可恢复有效订阅和股东会员。旧版的次数包余额保存在原设备。支持同步的版本通过 iCloud 读取本 App 的已购余额，请在原设备先打开支持同步的版本，并在两台设备使用相同的 iCloud 账户和 App Store 购买账户。「恢复购买」用于会员权益。

月度订阅按月自动续订，可在会员页管理订阅，或打开 iPhone/iPad「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」取消。取消续订后可使用至已付费周期结束；删除应用不会自动取消订阅。永久版不按月续费。

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

Card draws, card meanings, and reading history work offline. AI readings, follow-ups, and regeneration require a connection. From build 212, built-in readings go through the developer’s service at api.yubo.net. It checks access and forwards your question and the context needed for this reading to DeepSeek. Earlier builds connect directly to DeepSeek. Own-key readings connect directly to your configured provider. Apple product lookup, purchases, restoration, and entitlement updates may also require a connection.

How does the daily free allowance work?

Built-in AI includes 1 free request per day, reset at midnight Beijing time. A reading, follow-up, or regeneration counts once when it returns content. A failure with no content does not count.

Which memberships and reading packs are available?

Monthly membership renews automatically each month. While it is active, you have unlimited built-in readings and follow-ups and can connect your own provider.

Lifetime Patron is a one-time purchase. It permanently unlocks your own provider and keeps the daily free built-in reading. A Lifetime Patron who also holds an active monthly plan receives the Patron of Honor title. Lifetime access continues when the subscription ends. Your provider may charge separately.

Each reading pack adds 10 credits. You can buy more as needed. In builds that use the relay, active subscribers have unlimited built-in readings. Lifetime Patrons get one free built-in reading per day and can then switch to their own provider. Both keep all purchased credits. A free user can spend credits after using the daily free reading. Credits are held while membership status is uncertain. Credits do not expire and belong to this app. Versions with iCloud sync share the balance across devices using the same iCloud account and App Store purchase account. Earlier versions keep the balance on the original device. Apple shows the price for your region before purchase.

Purchases are separate for Fish Liu Ren and Fish Tarot. No free trial or Family Sharing is currently offered. Open More → Membership; AI settings and restricted result pages also link to membership.

Can I sync my reading history and drafts?

In versions with iCloud sync, subscribers and Lifetime Patrons can choose to sync their reading history, questions, follow-ups, drafts, notes and preferences to their private iCloud database. This setting is off by default. Personal provider keys stay on the device. Turning off content sync is independent of automatic purchased-credit syncing. Older versions continue to use local storage.

How do I configure my own AI?

With an active monthly subscription, or a Lifetime Patron purchase, open More → AI Assistant and add a model using your provider's service address, personal API key, and model name, then save and select it. The key stays on-device and is used only to authenticate with your configured service address. Built-in AI does not require this setup.

Where is AI data sent, and how can I withdraw permission?

When first using an AI service address that has not been permitted, including a new address, review the recipient and data scope shown in the app before choosing whether to allow the request. Readings and follow-ups send your question and relevant context; model and balance lookups also authenticate with your provider account. Declining or withdrawing does not affect offline features.

Open More → Data & Privacy to withdraw all AI data-sharing permissions. Further requests you initiate require permission again; automatic balance checks are skipped without network access when permission is absent. Withdrawal does not delete content already received by a provider. For deletion requests, use the contact details on this page or contact your own AI provider. See the Privacy Policy for retention and possible model training.

How do I restore purchases or manage a subscription?

Open Membership in More with the Apple account used for purchase. Restore Purchases restores active subscriptions and Lifetime Patron access. Earlier versions keep credit balances on the purchasing device. In a version with iCloud sync, open the updated app on the original device and use the same iCloud and App Store purchase accounts on both devices. Membership restoration and credit balance syncing are separate.

Monthly subscriptions renew automatically each month. Manage it from the membership page or open iPhone/iPad Settings → your Apple account → Subscriptions to cancel. After cancellation, access continues through the paid period. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase has no monthly renewal.
