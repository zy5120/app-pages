隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 16 日

小鱼塔罗（以下简称"本应用"）重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了我们如何处理您的信息。

数据收集

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人身份信息。

具体而言：

  • 占卜记录：所有占卜记录（抽牌结果、牌阵类型、AI 解读内容）仅存储在您的设备本地，不会上传至任何服务器。
  • API Key：如您使用 AI 解读功能，您配置的 API Key 仅存储在设备本地，直接连接至您选择的服务商（如 DeepSeek、通义千问等），不经过开发者服务器。
  • 个人信息：本应用不需要注册账号，不要求提供姓名、邮箱、手机号等任何个人信息。

数据使用

由于本应用不收集任何数据，我们不会使用、分享或出售您的任何信息。

第三方服务

  • AI 解读：您自行配置的 AI 服务商（DeepSeek / 通义千问 / 自定义）会收到您的占卜问题以生成解读。请参考各服务商各自的隐私政策。
  • App Store：本应用通过 Apple App Store 分发，Apple 可能根据其隐私政策收集某些信息。

本应用不含任何第三方分析、广告或跟踪组件。

数据安全

所有数据仅存储在您的设备上，受 iOS 系统级安全机制保护。删除本应用将同时删除所有本地数据。

用户权利

由于我们不持有您的任何数据：

  • 您可以在应用内自行删除占卜历史记录
  • 删除应用即删除所有数据
  • 如有其他隐私相关问题，请联系：Yuu5120@vip.qq.com

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，不故意收集儿童个人信息。

政策更新

如本隐私政策有重大变更，将在应用内通知。

联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请发送邮件至：Yuu5120@vip.qq.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Fish Tarot ("the App") values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

This app does NOT collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information.

  • Reading Records: All tarot readings (cards drawn, spreads, AI interpretations) are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • API Key: If you use the AI reading feature, your API Key is stored locally and connects directly to your chosen provider (e.g., DeepSeek, Qwen). It never passes through the developer's servers.
  • Personal Information: No account registration required. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any personal data.

Data Usage

Since we collect nothing, we use, share, or sell nothing.

Third-Party Services

  • AI Reading: Your chosen AI provider receives your query to generate readings. See their respective privacy policies.
  • App Store: Distributed via Apple's App Store. Apple may collect certain information per their privacy policy.

This app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking components.

Data Security

All data is stored solely on your device, protected by iOS system-level security. Deleting the app removes all local data.

Your Rights

  • You can delete your reading history anytime within the app
  • Deleting the app removes all data
  • For privacy inquiries: Yuu5120@vip.qq.com

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Policy Updates

Significant changes will be communicated within the app.

Contact

For privacy-related questions: Yuu5120@vip.qq.com
